Task BM · continuity layer

Chat history is not the same as task state.

Task BM is the product concept for remembering the evolving state of a service task: what was proposed, what both sides accepted, what is still pending, and what later changed or conflicted.

This is the core distinction from a simple chat + booking marketplace: important terms are intended to remain legible as the conversation evolves.

Research-informed moat

AI is being treated as a coordination layer—not only a generic chatbot or translator.

Founder-led R&D includes concepts around memory-conditioned continuity (AURA), functional status Z, context/resonance gating, and correction-aware contextual inference (including RGC / ACI-related work). The product goal is practical: preserve task state, detect ambiguity or meaningful change, and intervene with human confirmation.

Saudi first · GCC next

A market entry thesis built around communication and coordination.
